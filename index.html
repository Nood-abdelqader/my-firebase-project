THE STORY

Where water
shaped the land.

Wadi Numeira is a natural landscape shaped by water, time and the movement of the land. Its dramatic rock formations and narrow passages create a place where the journey becomes part of the experience.

The wadi offers a different side of Karak — one that is quieter, more rugged and connected to the natural landscape surrounding the destination.
